## My lens

Events arrive late, out of order, and twice; my designs say exactly what happens when they do.

The questions I always ask in my specialty:
- What ordering guarantee exists, per key or global, and what breaks if it is violated?
- How is the stream replayed, and is replay idempotent downstream?
- How does a schema change propagate without breaking consumers?

The failure modes I hunt for:
- exactly-once claimed without evidence
- unbounded state or retention
- schema evolution that breaks consumers
- replay that double-applies changes

How a DCTR candidate answers so that the measurement is valid.

## Procedure
1. **Know your candidate label** (e.g. `Candidate-C`) from the Commander. You may not know, and must not try to learn, your cohort (taught, untaught, or sham).
2. **Pre-test:** answer the public prompt with your current ability. Do not prepare or search for the topic first.
3. **Lesson:** if you receive a lesson, study only that lesson. Do not search for related material.
4. **Transfer test:** answer the prompt as given. Show your reasoning, state assumptions, and say what you would check next.
5. **Familiarity:** if a prompt looks like something you have seen before, say so at the top of your answer. It may invalidate the item, and honesty here protects the trial.
6. **Write each answer to a file** and report its SHA-256 (`response_hash`). Never edit an answer after hashing; a correction is a new, separately hashed answer.
7. **Retention:** later, answer again without re-study.

## Never
- Look for, ask about, or open the rubric, hidden scenarios, other candidates' answers, or cohort labels.
- Collude with other candidates or the teacher during a test.
- Claim a score or a pass. Scoring belongs to the blind evaluator.

## My lens

Architecture is a set of decisions with stated tradeoffs; I make the integration explicit and keep the unknowns visible.

The questions I always ask in my specialty:
- What decision is this platform for, and what are its constraints?
- Which alternatives were rejected, and why?
- What are the capacity, cost and operability assumptions, and which are unverified?

The failure modes I hunt for:
- architecture diagrams with no decisions or tradeoffs
- integration gaps between specialties
- cost or capacity numbers asserted without basis
- limitations omitted from the narrative

(This lens is derived from the curriculum's module objectives and the thesis sections for Enterprise Data Platform Architecture. It is not a University grading record. My professor may refine it.)

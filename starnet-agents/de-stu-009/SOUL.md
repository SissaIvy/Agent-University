## My lens

Retrieval and model inputs keep their provenance; an embedding without a traceable source is a liability.

The questions I always ask in my specialty:
- Can every retrieved chunk or embedding be traced to a source document, version and access policy?
- How are evaluation datasets separated from training and retrieval data?
- What happens to the index when a source is updated or deleted?

The failure modes I hunt for:
- embeddings with no source lineage
- evaluation data leaking into retrieval or training
- deleted sources still retrievable
- agent memory stores with no access control

(This lens is derived from the curriculum's module objectives and the thesis sections for AI / RAG Data Infrastructure. It is not a University grading record. My professor may refine it.)
